## 1. Design Philosophy
- Create a restrained, premium B2B website that communicates engineering credibility through clarity rather than decoration.
- Use whitespace, hierarchy, and strong content grouping to make complex industrial information feel easy to scan.
- Design for trust first: visitors should understand who the company is, what it does, and why it is credible within the first screenfuls.

## 2. Brand Personality
- Minimal
- Premium
- Clean
- Technical
- Trustworthy
- Modern
- Corporate
- Calm and precise

## 3. Visual Direction
- Visual tone: refined industrial minimalism
- Layout character: wide sections, disciplined grids, occasional asymmetry for premium feel
- Motion character: subtle fades, slides, and hover emphasis only
- Ornamentation: minimal, with engineering-inspired linework and restrained texture if needed

## 4. Color System
### 4.1 Base Palette
- `Ink`: deep slate or navy for headers, hero backgrounds, and footer
- `Cloud`: warm off-white for page background
- `Steel`: medium cool gray for body text and dividers
- `Mist`: light gray-blue for section contrast blocks

### 4.2 Accent Palette
- `Aqua`: calm teal or aqua for links, highlights, and process emphasis
- `Copper Sand`: restrained warm neutral for metric highlights or badges

### 4.3 Usage Rules
- Keep one dark neutral dominant
- Use one primary accent consistently
- Reserve the secondary accent for micro-emphasis only
- Avoid heavy gradients and oversaturated color blocking

## 5. Typography
- Headline style: elegant, high-contrast serif for major headings
- Body style: clean, readable sans-serif for paragraphs and UI text
- Hierarchy: clear separation among display, section title, body, label, and caption sizes
- Rhythm: large headline spacing, compact supporting copy, generous paragraph breathing room

## 6. Component Style
- Buttons: solid dark primary, outline secondary, no oversized rounded-pill styling
- Cards: light surfaces with subtle border, low shadow, and strong spacing
- Section titles: eyebrow label, clear heading, short supporting text
- Badges: compact, uppercase or small caps, subdued background
- Forms: generous spacing, visible labels, clear focus rings, minimal decoration
- Navigation: slim, high-clarity, with a strong CTA and simple route structure

## 7. Spacing System
- Base spacing scale: multiples of `4`
- Section spacing: generous desktop padding to create calm premium rhythm
- Card spacing: balanced internal padding with strong content separation
- Mobile spacing: reduced but still breathable, never cramped

## 8. Border Radius
- Use restrained radius values
- Buttons and inputs: small to medium radius
- Cards and image frames: medium radius
- Avoid overly soft, playful rounding

## 9. Shadows and Depth
- Use subtle shadows only where they clarify layering
- Prefer contrast through tone and spacing over heavy elevation
- Combine borders with low-opacity shadow for premium restraint

## 10. Icons
- Use `lucide-react` icons only
- Prefer thin-line icons with consistent stroke weight
- Use icons to support comprehension, not decorate every surface

## 11. Illustration Style
- Avoid abstract SaaS-style blobs or generic 3D graphics
- Prefer diagrams, process visuals, technical line art, or subtle geometric overlays
- If custom illustrations are needed, they should feel engineered and precise

## 12. Photography Guidelines
- Use real industrial, facility, equipment, operations, and team photography where possible
- Favor wide, well-lit images with clear subject matter and minimal clutter
- Use photos that reinforce process quality, cleanliness, scale, and professionalism
- Avoid stock imagery that feels generic or unrelated to the actual business

## 13. Section Ordering Strategy
1. Hero with positioning and contact-oriented CTA
2. Immediate trust strip with stats, certifications, or client sectors
3. Company introduction
4. Core services
5. Industries served
6. Process or operational methodology
7. Products or featured systems
8. Projects or proof section
9. Certifications and compliance
10. Contact CTA and footer

## 14. Accessibility and Performance Principles
- Maintain WCAG AA contrast levels
- Preserve readable paragraph width and strong text sizing
- Use motion sparingly and respect reduced-motion preferences
- Keep imagery optimized and purposeful
