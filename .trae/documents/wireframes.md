## 1. Purpose
This document captures Phase 4 wireframe-ready page structures for the `GSB INFRASTRUCTURE` website.

- Wireframes are content-agnostic structural blueprints
- All copy references are placeholders unless already confirmed
- Focus: layout order, content density, hierarchy, and CTA logic

## 2. Global Wireframe Rules
- Desktop-first layouts with mobile simplification
- Each page opens with a clear title or hero zone
- Each page must include one clear primary CTA and at least one supporting proof element
- Sections should alternate between narrative, proof, and action

## 3. Header and Footer Wireframe
### 3.1 Header
- Left: logo mark / wordmark placeholder
- Center or right: primary navigation
- Right: primary CTA button
- Mobile: menu button, logo, compact CTA if space allows

### 3.2 Footer
- Brand summary
- Quick links
- Services links
- Contact details
- Copyright and social links placeholder

## 4. Home Page Wireframe
### 4.1 Section Order
1. Hero
2. Trust strip
3. Company introduction
4. Core services
5. Industries served
6. Why choose us
7. Process
8. Featured products
9. Projects showcase
10. Certifications
11. CTA banner
12. Contact section

### 4.2 Home Layout Notes
- Hero: split layout with copy on left and image or structured visual on right
- Trust strip: compact horizontal band with metrics and badges
- Company intro: two-column text and image or proof block
- Services: four-card grid
- Industries: three or five-card grid depending width
- Why choose us: stat/proof plus short reasons list
- Process: horizontal step layout on desktop, vertical on mobile
- Products: image-led product cards
- Projects: featured card plus smaller supporting cards
- Certifications: badge grid with short supporting statement
- CTA banner: full-width contrasting block
- Contact: form and contact detail split layout
